**Effective Date:** April 7, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Monaco SMS notification service operated by Connor Frank.

## What This Service Does

Monaco is a personal automated trading portfolio assistant that sends SMS notifications about trade executions, portfolio summaries, and system status updates.

## Who Receives Messages

This service sends messages exclusively to the account owner (Connor Frank). No other individuals receive messages from this service.

## Information Collected

- **Phone number:** Used solely to deliver SMS notifications.
- **Message logs:** Twilio may retain message delivery logs per their own [privacy policy](https://www.twilio.com/legal/privacy).

No personal information is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties beyond what is necessary to deliver messages via Twilio.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on trading activity. Typical volume is 1–10 messages per day.

## Opt-Out

Reply **STOP** to any message to unsubscribe. Reply **START** to resubscribe. Message and data rates may apply.
